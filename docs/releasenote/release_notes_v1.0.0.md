The first release of SonderEye: the planet's live traffic on a native 3D globe, on your phone.

**On the globe**
- Earthquakes, aircraft with trails and follow mode, satellites with orbits and passes over you, ships, fire hotspots, public webcams, natural events and rain radar.
- Conflicts: places the news reports fighting in, with the stories, and an optional short account of what happened (Gemini, your own key).
- Bus lines and stops from OpenStreetMap, each line in its own colour. Pick a line to see its route, or ride it: the map follows you and says the next stop.
- Live buses where the operator publishes them (Transitland, your own free key).
- Surveillance cameras mapped in OpenStreetMap.
- Satellite imagery from space down to your street, with sharp roads drawn by the app close in, day and night with city lights.

**Around it**
- Today: the weather where you are and the day's news, with an optional written summary.
- Sky view: point the phone at the sky to see what is above you.
- ISS pass alerts, search, and a nearest-first list for every layer, where any layer can be hidden.

**Notes**
- Android 8.0 or newer. No account and no server of ours; keys and settings stay on the phone.
- Optional free keys (ships, webcams, fires, live buses, written summaries) go in the menu's API keys section.

**Windows (new)**
- SonderEye now also runs on Windows 10 and 11 as a native app: the same globe, layers, panels and data, from the same code.
- `SonderEye-1.0.0-Setup.exe` installs it (no administrator rights needed); `SonderEye-1.0.0-Portable.zip` runs from a folder without installing.
- Mouse: drag to move, wheel to zoom, right-drag to turn, right-click for the weather and to set your place. Touchpad: slide two fingers to move, pinch to zoom. F11 leaves full screen.
- Your place comes from your internet connection until you set it. The sky view and bus riding are phone-only.
- The .exe is not code-signed: Windows SmartScreen asks once ("More info", then "Run anyway").
