SonderEye 1.1.0, for Android and Windows.

- Dots: pick your own colour and size for fires, conflicts, earthquakes, natural events and cameras (menu, Pins). Default brings back their own colours.
- The menu is tidier: settings are folded into groups (Map, Pins, On the ground, Air space and sea, Buses, API keys, App) that open on a tap, one at a time, with how many layers are on shown next to each.
- Today: the written summary is about the news only; the weather is already shown above it.

**Downloads**: `SonderEye-1.1.0.apk` (Android 8.0+), `SonderEye-1.1.0-Setup.exe` or `SonderEye-1.1.0-Portable.zip` (Windows 10/11). Install over the previous version; settings and keys stay.
