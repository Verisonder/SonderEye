SonderEye 1.0.1, for Android and Windows.

**Both**
- Pin size in the menu (Map section): Small, Medium or Large. The phone keeps its size (Large) until you change it.

**Windows**
- Opens truly full screen, with no title bar; F11 switches to a window and back, Alt+F4 closes.
- Touchpad: slide two fingers to move the map, pinch to zoom.
- Much smoother with bus lines on, and with the street roads close in.
- Less memory used over time.
- Pins start at Medium, better suited to a PC screen.

**Downloads**: `SonderEye-1.0.1.apk` (Android 8.0+), `SonderEye-1.0.1-Setup.exe` or `SonderEye-1.0.1-Portable.zip` (Windows 10/11). Install over the previous version; settings and keys stay.
