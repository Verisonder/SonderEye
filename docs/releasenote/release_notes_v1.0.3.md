SonderEye 1.0.3, for Android and Windows.

- Bus stops are now a white bus on a blue sign, bigger and easier to tap or click. A picked line's stops take its colour.
- Windows keyboard: W A S D (or the arrows) move the map, E picks the point nearest the middle of the screen (E again for the next), Enter opens it.

**Downloads**: `SonderEye-1.0.3.apk` (Android 8.0+), `SonderEye-1.0.3-Setup.exe` or `SonderEye-1.0.3-Portable.zip` (Windows 10/11). Install over the previous version; settings and keys stay.
